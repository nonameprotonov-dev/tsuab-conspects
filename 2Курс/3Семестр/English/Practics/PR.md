### INTERNET VOCABULARY QUIZ

Match the words with their meanings:

1. WWW — J. an interconnection of computer information systems available via the Internet (World Wide Web)
2. E-mail — C. a letter you send through your computer
3. Internet address — K. another way to say URL
4. URL — G. an internet address (Uniform Resource Locator)
5. Chat Room — A. a place where you talk by typing
6. Virus — D. a hidden program that can hurt your computer
7. Password — B. a secret word that only you and your parents know
8. SPAM — E. advertising that comes to your e-mail address
9. Blog — O. a journal you keep on the Web, short for Web log
10. Screen Name — F. a name you choose for yourself that isn't your real name
11. Internet — M. a worldwide system of computers
12. Website — I. a place on the Internet you can visit
13. Cyberspace — L. the world that only exists in the computer and your head
14. Username — N. another way to say "username"
15. Download — H. a process of file transfer from the Internet

### INTERNET CONVERSATION QUESTIONS

Warm-up Question:
**Which website do you visit most often? What do you do there?**

**Discussion Questions:**

**1. How many hours per day do you spend online? Is it too many?**
*I spend about 16 hours per day online. It's too much.*

**2. What is your preferred search engine? Why is it the best?**
*My preferred search engine is Google. Google is the most stable and popular search engine, and Yandex promotes viruses.*

**3. How often do you use search engines? What do you search for?**
*Every day. Documentation about the C++ language.*

**4. There is a lot of information on the Internet. How do you know which information is accurate?**
*No way.*

**5. Do you worry about online security? What are the risks of being online?**
*I don't worry about that because I know the basic rules of using the Internet. Nothing more than scammers.*

**6. How old should a child be before they are allowed to use the Internet?**
*1-3 years old, but chatting on the Internet should be allowed after 16 years old.*

**7. Do you know anyone who never uses the Internet? Why don't they use it?**
*No. I don't have such a friend.*

**8. Do you have any experience running a blog or building a website? Talk about it.**
*No, I don't have experience.*

**9. By using the Internet, do you feel connected? Connected to what or whom?**
*Yes, I feel connected to my friends and the world news.*

**10. Is your Internet fast enough? How do you feel when the Internet is working slowly?**
*Yes, my Internet is fast enough. When it is slow, I feel very angry and nervous*

**11. When was the last time you had no Internet access? What were you doing?**
*The last time was when I was sleeping or reading a paper book. It was calm.*

**12. Do you do online shopping? What do you like to buy online and what do you prefer to buy in a store?**
*Yes, I buy electronics online. But I prefer to buy food in a store because I want to see it.*

**13. What social media do you use these days? Why do you prefer those over other social networks?**
*I use Telegram and VK. I prefer them because they are fast and easy to use on my phone.*

**14. Do you watch a lot of YouTube? What kinds of content do you watch?**
*Yes, I watch YouTube a lot. I watch videos about programming, C++, and sometimes games.*

**15. What is your favorite smartphone app? What do you do with it?**
*My favorite app is Telegram. I chat with friends and read news there.*

**16. Have you played online games? What did you play?**
*Yes, I have played online games. I played Minecraft with my friends.*

**17. Have you tried online dating? What is good and bad about online dating?**
*No, I haven't tried online dating. I think it is bad because people can lie about themselves.*

**18. Have you ever met someone online and then met them in person? Was the person like you expected them to be?**
*Yes, I met my friend from an online game. He was normal, like I expected.*

**19. Many services are online these days. Which parts of our lives will never be online?**
*Medicine and sports will never be online. You need a real doctor and a real gym.*

**20. Are you interested in online business? Have you thought about starting your own? In which area?**
*Yes, I am interested. I want to start an online business in IT.*
